## Platform

## Users

Primary: schools, educational institutions and businesses (offices) that want stationery, office essentials and document services from one nearby supplier. Secondary: walk-in locals on Horamavu Agara Main Road, Bangalore (parents, students, small offices). Most visitors are likely on phones, so calling must be one tap.

## Product Purpose

Grace Stationery is a stationery and xerox shop. It stocks stationery, school and office supplies, art and craft materials, gifts, novelties and toys under one roof, with xerox, lamination and spiral binding at the counter. The site is a marketing page: it helps people understand what the shop does, lists what the services cost, and brings them into direct contact. Actual business happens by talking directly (phone, WhatsApp, email or walking in); there is no online checkout. Success is a call, a message or a visit.

## Positioning

One shop for school and office supplies plus counter services (xerox, lamination, spiral binding), with posted prices, on a single local road.

## Operating Context

Enquiries and orders start by phone, WhatsApp, email or in person, and WhatsApp and email are first points of entry that arrive with the shop directly. There is no online checkout and no contact form or backend; email works through `mailto:` links.

## Product Principles

1. Reach the shop quickly: phone, hours and address are never more than a tap away.
2. Show real prices and real categories; never fabricate proof or claims.
3. Speak to institutional buyers first without losing the walk-in neighbour.
4. One shop, one road: keep it local and plainspoken.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
