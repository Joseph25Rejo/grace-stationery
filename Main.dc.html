About us

Your neighbourhood paper people

Grace Stationery is a stationery and xerox shop on Horamavu Agara Main Road in Bangalore. We keep stationery, school and office supplies, art and craft materials, gifts, novelties and toys under one roof, with xerox, lamination and spiral binding at the counter.

Students, parents, teachers and office teams are all welcome. Yes, we will laminate that certificate. No, we will not comment on the photo.
